Where to start

Three common situations

We are getting started

You want to use AI or modernize how you work, and you are unsure where the value is. We find the two or three uses that matter, and start with the smallest one that proves itself.

Our pilots have not paid off

You have demos and experiments, and little in daily use. We find out why they stalled, pick what deserves to continue, and take it to production with a measure of success agreed first.

Our AI-built software is not holding together

Agents produce a lot, and the project gets harder to understand and change. We restore the structure that lets your team explain, change and verify the system as it grows.

Approach

Why human-centered

Technology projects fail when the people responsible for them can no longer explain what is happening or judge whether a change made things better. AI makes this easier to fall into, because it produces work faster than people can absorb it. Every engagement is built to keep that understanding intact.

  1. UnderstandHow the organization works now, in its own words.
  2. ChooseOne step small enough to check and valuable enough to matter.
  3. DeliverBuild it, with the checks agreed before the work starts.
  4. MeasureCompare the result with the goal, then decide the next step.
